# Vault Wiki — root index

Agent-maintained knowledge bundle. Start here; drill down via links. Regenerated by the pipeline — do not hand-edit.

# Sections

* [Concepts](concepts/) - research brain: ideas, methods, technical and world knowledge.
* [Projects](projects/) - ongoing work: one hub page per project plus satellites.
* [People](people/) - collaborators and contacts: who they are, context, history.
* [Life](life/) - personal facts and infrastructure agents need to act on the user's behalf.
* [Craft](craft/) - craft knowledge: how the user works right now — workflows, principles, stack defaults. Fast-evolving; efforts themselves live in Projects.
* [Connections](connections/) - first-class claims that two things relate; the insight surface between notes and wiki.
* [Log](log/) - dated history of what the pipeline did; skim, don't read whole.
