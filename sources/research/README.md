# What this folder is

The permanent home for research the maintainer produced itself: web research passes, live-page reads, site re-checks, verification sweeps. Moved here from inbox/ by `ingest`. Minted 2026-09-04 at the user's direction after three batches had filed into articles/ under a `modality:` override ("'articles' isn't quite the right name", the user, 2026-08-06).

# Boundary

Belongs here: anything where the item is the agent's own compiled findings from external surfaces. Stays in articles/: a copy, transcript, or outline of one external document, however the agent captured it, with a `modality:` line if the capture form needs saying. Items filed to articles/ before this stream existed stay where they are; paths are permanent.

# Rules

- **Frozen once filed.** Never edit or delete a filed source — it is a record of what was found, not a truth claim. A wrong finding is outweighed or disputed at the wiki layer, never "fixed" here. <!-- constitution: principle 2, freeze speech -->
- Filenames are permanent: provenance in wiki pages cites these paths forever.
- Every claim in a filed item carries its URL or surface and its read date.
- [index.md](index.md) is derived; the pipeline regenerates it.

# Evidential modality

A filed research item evidences **"the agent found X on the cited surfaces when it looked"**: what each cited page, listing, or filing showed at read time, compiled by the maintainer. Never that X is true, and never that a source said it (the item is the agent's report, not a copy of any one document). Live pages drift, so the read date is part of the claim. Wiki claims built from this stream cite the item and the surface it read, and land as `draft`.
